What’s new.

Versioned releases, clear changes, and a small app that stays useful.

1.0.0 beta 5 · November 4, 2026

Your history. Without repeated sign-ins.

Each installation now gets its own private backup identity automatically. No account or owner password is needed. Save a recovery file outside the Mac to restore after deleting all app data.

  • Normal builds open the local-first app; legacy owner builds are distinctly named.
  • Fresh recovery restores historical goals and resumes backup at the correct revision.
  • Existing histories are never silently mixed or duplicated.

1.0.0 beta 4 · November 4, 2026

A more compact Rhythm.

The activity heat map is now 15% smaller, including its tiles, labels, controls, and tooltip. Its layout height shrinks too. Six months load initially, with earlier months available in six-month batches.

Includes all beta 3 history protection, private cloud backup, clock settings, and compact dashboard improvements.

1.0.0 beta 3 · November 4, 2026

A clearer rhythm. A safer history.

  • Rhythm heat map with green monthly tiles, six months per load, daily breakdowns, and 20-Easy-Apply streaks.
  • Compact Home and Usage layouts, six rolling statistics, and two-line entry dates.
  • India time by default, configurable time zones, and 12/24-hour clocks.
  • General, Alerts, and Data settings tabs.
  • Independent local safety copies and optional private cloud backup, with separate local/cloud status indicators.
  • Reviewed recovery preserves existing entries, deletions, and current goals.

Local use remains account-free. Cloud backup requires a private Vigil workspace you own. Public downloads contain no personal records or credentials.

1.0.0 beta 2 · October 22, 2026

No more login Keychain prompts.

Removed the legacy Keychain session integration. The public app remains local-only and account-free after quitting or restarting your Mac.

  • No reads, writes, or deletion of saved owner-session Keychain items; release checks reject those native calls.
  • Correct native website links, clearer sign-in wording for the separate connected build, and unused-code cleanup.
  • Malformed day edits now return a validation message. Local build/install commands protect running apps and retain rollback copies.
  • Existing public local records remain in place when you quit the app and replace it with the new version. Export a JSON backup before updating.

The connected owner build keeps its login only while open and needs a new owner sign-in after quitting. Public downloads never require an owner account. This beta is still ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS first-launch approval remains a separate step.

1.0.0 beta 1 · First public beta

Your applications, in the menu bar.

The first public release brings Apply Vigil to macOS 13 and later, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

  • Local use from first launch, with no account or internet required.
  • Menu bar counts, native glass dashboard, and a movable floating compact window.
  • Easy Apply and external/form quick counts, custom counts, mixed batches, goals, and undo.
  • Daily charts, UTC history, compact entry rows, reviewed corrections, averages, and streaks.
  • Quiet progress alerts with six sound choices and Silent.
  • JSON backup and restore, plus CSV export.
  • Optional notifications and launch at login.

This beta is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. Updates are manual. Follow the installation guide for the first open.
