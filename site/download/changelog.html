1.0.0 beta 1 · First public beta

Your applications, in the menu bar.

The first public release brings Apply Vigil to macOS 13 and later, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

  • Local use from first launch, with no account or internet required.
  • Menu bar counts, native glass dashboard, and a movable floating compact window.
  • Easy Apply and external/form quick counts, custom counts, mixed batches, goals, and undo.
  • Daily charts, UTC history, compact entry rows, reviewed corrections, averages, and streaks.
  • Quiet progress alerts with six sound choices and Silent.
  • JSON backup and restore, plus CSV export.
  • Optional notifications and launch at login.

This beta is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. Updates are manual. Follow the installation guide for the first open.
